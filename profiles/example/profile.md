# Alex Rivera — what the ranker judges against

**This is a fictional example.** Copy the folder (`cp -r profiles/example
profiles/me`) and rewrite every line as yourself. `profiles/me` is gitignored.

Kept deliberately short. This is the yardstick a local model holds each posting
against, not a CV. The CV facts live in the `cv-builder` skill and in
`reference/`.

## Target

- **Role: Machine Learning Engineer.** Applied ML and LLM systems in
  production, evaluation, MLOps. Data Scientist roles with real deployment work
  count. Research-only roles do not.
- **Location: Berlin.** On site or hybrid in Berlin is best, remote within
  Germany is fine, remote elsewhere in the EU is acceptable.
- **Language: English.** German is A2: a nice-to-have is fine, a requirement at
  B2 and above is a blocker.

## Experience

About three years professional. ML engineer at a mid-size logistics company:
demand forecasting models in production, a retrieval pipeline over internal
documents, and the evaluation harness that gates every model release.

Strong: Python, PyTorch, scikit-learn, SQL, FastAPI, Docker, model evaluation,
feature pipelines, experiment tracking.

## Not their lane

Computer vision, embedded ML, frontend-led roles, pure DevOps or SRE, and data
annotation or AI-training contractor work.

## The two hard rules

1. **Years.** Out only when the description itself states five or more years
   required. A number quoted for any other reason does not count.
2. **Titles are noise.** "Senior" or "Lead" alone is not a blocker; only roles
   whose actual job is managing a team and owning headcount are out.
